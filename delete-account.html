Account deletion

Delete your QuickSync account

This page deletes a QuickSync P2P Transfer account and the data linked to it. You can use it without installing the app, for example after uninstalling it.

Delete from inside the app

Open QuickSync, tap the account chip in the header, then choose Delete account and confirm. The app deletes the account immediately and signs this device out.

Delete from the web

Sign in below with the same email and password you use in the app. The account is deleted immediately; there is no waiting period.

What is deleted: your email address, display name, password hash or Google sign-in link, every device registered to the account (device name, model, public key and last-seen time), and every sign-in session. The account cannot be restored.

What is kept

Files you transferred are never stored on the server, so there is nothing to delete there. The signaling server keeps short-lived operational logs (IP address, request path and sign-in events) for security and abuse prevention; these are not part of your account and are rotated by the operator. See the QuickSync privacy policy for details.

Signed in with Google?

If you created the account with Google and never set a password, use the in-app Delete account option, or email olksur@gmail.com from the address on the account and we will delete it for you.
