Privacy Policy — PairPort P2P Transfer

Last updated: November 20, 2026

PairPort P2P Transfer ("PairPort", "the app") transfers files and text directly between your devices. This policy explains what the optional account feature collects, why, and how you can delete it.

What the app collects

Transfers are peer-to-peer. File contents and clipboard text are sent directly to the other device (or through the signaling relay when a direct connection is not possible) and are never stored on the server. The account feature collects only:

  • Account data: email address, display name, and a password hash (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256) or, when you sign in with Google, the Google account subject identifier and email address.
  • Device directory: a random device id, device name, model, role, the device's long-term public key, the signaling server URL, and the last time the device was online.
  • Operational logs: IP address, request path, timestamps and sign-in events, kept for security and abuse prevention.

Why it is collected

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in across your devices.
  • To show your devices in "Previously Paired Devices" with live online status, so one device can ask another to reconnect.
  • To protect the service against abuse and to diagnose failures.

What is shared

We do not sell your data and do not share it with advertisers. Data is processed by:

  • Google Sign-In, if you choose it, to verify your identity. Google's privacy policy applies to that step.
  • Our hosting providers, which run the signaling server and serve this website.

Retention

Account and device records are kept until you delete your account. Operational logs are kept for a limited period for security and abuse prevention and are rotated by the operator. Transferred files are not stored on the server at all.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time:

  • In the app: open the account chip in the header, choose Delete account, and confirm.
  • On the web: use the account deletion page. It works without installing the app.

Deletion removes your email address, display name, password hash or Google link, every device registered to the account, and every sign-in session. It cannot be undone.

Security

Passwords are stored only as PBKDF2 hashes. Account traffic uses HTTPS/WSS. Pairing and transfers use ECDH P-256 key agreement and AES-256-GCM encryption between the two devices. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

PairPort is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
